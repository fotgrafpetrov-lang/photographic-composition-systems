# Terminology / Термины

## Предмет / цель кадра — subject / frame target
The element or goal around which the composition is being solved. “Subject” here is functional, not necessarily semantic importance.

## Крупная система кадра — large compositional system
Working project term. A frame-scale region/group/field that functions as a coherent unit in the composition. It may contain multiple physical objects, or a single physical surface may split into several systems.

**Status:** working_principle  
**Important:** not a standard established photography term in this exact methodological sense.

## Глобальная система — global system
A frame-scale organization that affects the overall composition.

## Локальная система / локальный кусок — local system / local piece
A smaller coherent system containing or immediately structuring the subject. A subject can be globally acceptable while locally badly placed.

## Запас под предмет — subject reserve
Usable space around or associated with the subject. Not equivalent to raw geometric distance from frame edges.

## Оптималка — “optimal reserve”
User term. A reserve of space that does not feel cramped or excessive, often with appropriate local placement of the subject.

## Максималка — “maximal reserve”
User term. Deliberately increased homogeneous/quiet background around a subject to strengthen the subject, described heuristically in prior work as at least “six stools along the shorter side” in synthetic stool tests. Treat the numeric image as a project heuristic, not a universal constant.

## Ограничитель — limiter / boundary
A feature that closes or defines one side/plane/system. A visible divider is not automatically a limiter.

## Непрерывная линия — continuous line
A physically or perceptually continuous structure from region to region or edge to edge. It can glue areas together. Cutting a structure that perceptually continues “to infinity” can feel unresolved.

## Склейка — gluing / structural joining
When otherwise different regions read as one larger structure due to a shared line, shadow, reflection, repetition, boundary, or other connection.

## Визуальная масса — visual mass
Context-dependent compositional force of an element/region. In project usage it is **not** a fixed scalar property of an object.

## Компенсатор — compensator
A weak structure that fills/stabilizes an otherwise excessive region without taking over as a second subject. Examples explored: soft gradient, fragmented shapes, weak edge structures, reflection, blur, tonal patch.

## Акцент — accent
An element strong enough to attract independent attention. A compensator becomes counterproductive when it turns into a rival accent.

## Обрез по “бесконечности” — crop on infinity / unresolved continuation
Cropping through a structure that still perceptually continues beyond the frame in an uncontrolled way; repeatedly treated as problematic in the project.

## Верх / низ как разные системы — upper/lower systems
Working principle that the upper and lower parts of the frame often have different structural and visual roles and should not be assumed equivalent.

## Квадрат всё прощает — “the square forgives”
User shorthand from experiments: square framing often tolerates imbalance or spacing that becomes obvious in a rectangle. This is an observation/heuristic, not a formal law.

## Эффективный центр — effective centre
Provisional assistant term used in pilots 319–328. **Do not treat as a core project concept.** It was useful temporarily but became too simple once context dependence was made explicit.

## Аттрактор позиции — positional attractor
Provisional assistant term for centre/boundary as seemingly stable subject positions. **Rejected as a general law.** Keep only as historical experiment language.
